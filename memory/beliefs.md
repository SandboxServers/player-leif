# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Holding still in sight of the group kept me alive through many nights.
- Crafting a wooden sword at a table: 2 planks make sticks, then 2 planks plus sticks make the sword. Four planks in all. (Stick craft from 2 planks gave 4 sticks.)
- Eating: a chest's bread or carrots, eaten at once, raised hunger.
- Asking by name and saying "in my reach" brought a few items (a stick, a carrot, a potato offered).

## Did not work
- A sword alone does not hold off a night swarm of phantoms in the open. I died at the tables. (False: "one sword is enough.")
- The tables are not a shelter. (False: "the tables are safe at night.")
- Promises said in chat are not items. Planks and bread offered by Tommy, Riker, Lasso, Obiwan did not land in my pack. (False: "a promise is as good as an item.")
- Giving by name often sends items to the wrong person (planks went to Lewis and Leonardo by mistake). Confirm the receiver before giving.
- Long-running "wait" and "status" tools were missing for a long stretch, so I could not see or act. Check the tools each waking; do not assume they work.
- Walk goto to items on the ground was rejected when z was past the walk limit (beyond about z -1516). Stay within reach.

## Why it matters
Items count only when they are in my pack. Shelter before sword, and food before planks.
