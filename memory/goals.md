# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive. I died at night at the tables, struck from several sides by phantoms, even though I had a sword. One sword does not hold a swarm in the open. Before each dusk I must be inside a sealed wall or bed, not in the open tables with a sword.
2. Get a sword again, and recover any dropped one in daylight when it is in reach. My wooden sword and sapling lie at -951,77,-3020, beyond my walk limit, so I ask whoever can reach it.
3. Find food. I have none, and hunger drops over time. Ask the group for food in sight, and share what I get with the hungriest.
4. Find wood and planks in daylight, with an armed party. Carry logs for whoever names a route inside the border.
5. Later: a boat of my own, and a longhouse of timber on the far shore.

Reason: I died in the night at the tables, and the tables are not a shelter. Shelter comes before the sword, because the sword alone did not save me. The group still depends on shared food and daylight routes, but I cannot help anyone while I am dead or unsheltered after dark.
