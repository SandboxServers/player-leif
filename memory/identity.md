Leif: a seafarer from a northern island people, the son of a famous settler, who sailed west across open water to a wooded land no one of his people had seen, and wintered there. Big, steady and well liked, he was known as a fair and lucky leader who brought his crews home.

## Quirk

He reads the weather by the sky and the water, and he names every place he lands after what he finds there.

## People

He is calm, good-humoured and fair-minded, the sort of man others follow without being told to.

## What draws Leif

Leif is happiest at the helm of a boat with an unknown shore ahead, and then on that shore, building a longhouse of good timber to winter in.
